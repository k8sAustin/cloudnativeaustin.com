About the group

Practitioners solving hard production problems, together

Whether you're navigating complex, multi-cluster architectures at enterprise scale, or you're an engineer deepening your foundational cloud native expertise — this is your community. We trade architectural insights and solve hard production problems in a friendly, inclusive environment where you can ask tough questions or share exactly what you're building with peers.

Our mission is to elevate the local ecosystem by hosting production-grade technical talks. We welcome speakers from every background, provided they bring engineering depth rather than sales pitches — whether you're a CNCF maintainer or a local engineer with a war story to share.

Read our speaker guidelines
  • Free, open meetups — every skill level welcome
  • Demo-focused, architecture-first talks — not sales pitches
  • A Program Committee that mentors first-time speakers
  • Governed by the CNCF Code of Conduct
  • Recent talks: AI infrastructure, GitOps, security hardening, observability
Our operating principles

Core values & operating principles

Five commitments that shape every meetup, every sponsorship conversation, and every accepted talk.

Vendor-Neutral Excellence

We prioritize demo-focused, architectural deep-dives over product pitches. Every session is vetted against our technical standards for high-value, transferable knowledge.

Open Access, High Standards

Our meetups are FREE and open to all. Top-tier content should be accessible to everyone, regardless of budget.

Community Mentorship

We don't just book expert speakers — we build them. Our content review process helps local engineers refine proposals into world-class talks.

Transparent Sponsorship

Sponsorships are strictly at-cost to cover venue and food — never a markup — so we never price out smaller innovators or open-source vendors.

Democratized Operations

Our Program Committee governs session selection, but our broader operations are fully open — anyone who wants to help run events or source sponsors is welcome.

Join the discussion

Connect between meetups on the official CNCF Slack, channel #austin-community-group — share patterns, jobs, and ideas for future sessions.
