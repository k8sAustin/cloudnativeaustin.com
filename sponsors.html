How it works

An at-cost, in-kind-friendly model

Strictly at-cost

Financial sponsorship covers venue and food only — there's no premium tier that buys extra marketing reach or influence over content.

In-kind counts too

Any organization that donates venue space or provides high-quality speakers is automatically recognized as an official sponsor — no check required.

Vendor-neutral, always

Sponsorship never buys stage time. Every talk — sponsored or not — goes through the same Program Committee review for engineering depth.
